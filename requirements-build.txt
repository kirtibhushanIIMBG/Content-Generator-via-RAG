# --- PDF extraction. BOTH are load-bearing; see the docstring in src/ingest/chunk.py.
#
# pdfminer.six — body text. It resolves the Act's nested coordinate space and its 8pt
#   margin side-notes, which flat extraction injects into the middle of legal sentences
#   ("...only in accordance PROCESSING with the provisions..."). Also the only one that
#   reads page 1, whose body sits inside a Form XObject.
# pypdf — Schedules, via extraction_mode="layout". It is the only one that keeps the
#   penalty table's rows aligned; read any other way the table comes out in column order
#   and every penalty comes unstuck from its breach.
#
# Neither alone gets both right. Do not consolidate.
pdfminer.six
pypdf
