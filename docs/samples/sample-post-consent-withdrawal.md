A patient withdrew consent last week.

Your hospital is still processing their data. And you don't know it.

Under the DPDP Act, a patient can withdraw consent at any time — and it must be as easy to withdraw
as it was to give¹. The moment that email arrives, every use beyond their care loses its lawful basis.

Not next billing cycle. From that moment.

Most hospitals have never built the three things that make this possible:

→ a withdrawal that lands as a workflow event, not a lost inbox message
→ a map of every system the data reached — HIS, TPA, CRM, analytics
→ proof that processing actually stopped, and when

You cannot stop what you cannot see.

This isn't enforced until 13 May 2027. But the exposure is being built into your systems today.

Could you prove you stopped? Right now?
